# Weapon X: Wolverine

> *"They tried to erase the man and build a weapon. All they left behind was the beast."*

* **Theme:** Marvel Studio (OpenGL 3D RPG Project)
* **Inspiration:** *Wolverine: Weapon X* by Barry Windsor-Smith

## Game Objective

**Escape the Weapon X lab.** Break free from containment, fight through the underground facility's defenses, and make it out alive into the Canadian wilderness.

## Story Overview

### 1. Capture & The Experiment
Living in isolation in the Canadian wilderness, **Logan**—a mutant possessing retractable bone claws and a rapid healing factor—is ambushed and drugged by **Department K**. Dragged into a hidden underground laboratory, he is stripped of his humanity and treated strictly as a military *"specimen"* by three scientists: **The Professor (Thorton)**, **Dr. Abraham Cornelius**, and **Carol Hines**. Submerged in a liquid tank, Logan endures the agonizing surgical bonding of indestructible **Adamantium** to his skeleton, surviving only because his healing factor repairs his body as fast as the metal destroys it.

### 2. Fractured Reality & Berserker Rage
To erase his identity and test his combat efficiency, the scientists flood Logan's mind with false memories and virtual simulations. Driven past the brink of sanity by relentless physical and psychological torture, his primal instincts finally shatter the mind-control implants. Logan breaks out of his tank in a pure, unstoppable **berserker rage**, turning the sterile facility into a blood-splattered slaughterhouse as he hunts down the guards and executes his tormentors.

### 3. Into the Cold
Escaping the facility into the freezing Canadian wilderness, Logan emerges bleeding, traumatized, and stripped of his memories. Leaving his human past behind, the broken survivor officially becomes the feral beast known as **Wolverine**.
